It contains no nvd_status, n_affected_cpes, n_references, CVSS/blank-score pattern, or references.csv field. The TF-IDF vocabulary is fitted on train rows only. - **Label permutation (train labels shuffled, whole pipeline refit, 20 draws).** Test AUC mean 0.481, SD 0.122, range 0.22..0.73. Top-500 hits 0..4. The null is centred at 0.5. The real 0.888 is above all 20 draws (about 3.4 SD). No label leak was detected. The null is wide, so differences of about 0.1 AUC between variants on one seed are not by themselves strong evidence.

## 5. Leaks and design problems

1. **Training labels overlap the test period (time leak in the backtest design, not a bug).** The primary model's 2024 training labels use KEV additions through 2025-12-31, the same calendar window in which the test labels are realised. 25 of the 65 train positives come from 2025. Section B removes this: top500 25 -> 21, AUC .888 -> .871. The final 2026 model will have no such overlap, because labels end 2025-12-31 and the scoreboard starts 2026-01. **B is therefore the honest analogue of the 2026 setting, and the primary numbers are optimistic.**
2. **EPSS coverage drifts inside the test period.** EPSS is missing for 44.7% of 2025-01 rows and 23.3% of 2025-02 rows, versus 0.5-2.4% for 2025-04..09. EPSS also changed model in March 2025. Not a leak, but it confounds the EPSS baseline and any month-wise comparison.
3. **`description` is today's text.** H shows 55 test rows with exploitation wording (11% of them positive, against a 0.15% base rate). It does not drive the result here, but it is an unmitigated risk for the 2026 ranking. Recommended: mask the H pattern before TF-IDF.
4. **Survivor effect.** Rejected CVEs are absent from today's file. This is documented and not fixable from this data.
5. **Assigner history counts only CVEs published 2021+.** This is a documented design choice, not a leak.
6. **Text-score distribution shift.** Train rows get OOF text scores; test rows get a full-train fit. This is standard stacking and not a leak.

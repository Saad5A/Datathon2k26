## PRE-REGISTRATION, Phase 3 (written before any Phase 3 result was computed) | feat/model-ranking | 2026-11-07 16:55 | orchestrator
- **Evaluation:** two folds, both with time-safe train labels (KEV additions up to the train end only), which mirrors the 2026 setup. Fold 1: train 2024-01..12 (labels to 2024-12-31), test 2025-01..09. Fold 2: train 2024-07..2025-03 (labels to 2025-03-31), test 2025-04..09. Test labels as in the harness. Ties broken by cve_id; tie-aware expected top-500 also reported.
- **Common settings:** exploitation wording (harness `EXPLOIT_WORDING`) masked out of descriptions before any text model. TF-IDF and the text LR fitted on train rows only, with 5-fold out-of-fold scores for train rows. HGB: depth 3, lr 0.05, max_iter 150, min leaf 40, L2 5, early_stopping=False. 5 seed sets (seed s for both the HGB and the text folds); the median over seeds is reported, with the range.
- **Candidates (only these three):** C1 = HGB on signals + all-history assigner rate/count (as in the harness) + masked text score. C2 = HGB on signals + 24-month-window assigner rate/count (KEV additions and CVEs published in the 24 months before the snapshot month) + masked text score. C3 = L2 logistic regression on the C2 features (standardised; blank EPSS = 0 plus a missing indicator).
- **Selection rule:** the highest mean AUC over the two folds (seed medians). If a candidate is within 0.01 of the best, prefer it in this order: C3 (simplest, easiest to explain), C2 (recent window, more robust to the 2026 shift), C1. Top-500, top-250, average precision, median rank and work-to-catch-50% are reported but NOT used to select, because with 32-52 positives top-500 swings by about 4 hits from seeds alone and reversed between folds.
- **After selection only:** ablations on the chosen model (drop signals / assigner / text; unmasked text; + CVSS/CWE structured features; early stopping on), unseen-assigner subset, label-permutation test, paired bootstrap vs exploit-then-EPSS.
